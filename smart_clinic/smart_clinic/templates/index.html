Introduction

Chhote clinics me aaj bhi patient ki file, appointment ki diary aur bill alag alag register me hote hain. Is project me hamne yeh sab ek system me jodh diya hai aur uske upar analytics laga di hai, taki doctor aur clinic owner faisle data dekh kar le sakein.

Project scope

System chaar hisson me banta hai.

Patient records

Naam, umar, shehar, phone aur visit history ek profile me.

Appointments

Doctor aur department ke hisab se booking, cancel aur no-show ka record.

Billing

Consultation, lab aur medicine charges ka bill, Cash, UPI, Card ya Insurance se payment.

Health-data analytics

Pandas se calculation, Matplotlib aur Seaborn se charts.

Analytics from the clinic data

Neeche ke charts is project ke apne generate kiye hue dataset se bane hain.

Built with Python, Flask, NumPy, Pandas, Matplotlib and Seaborn.
